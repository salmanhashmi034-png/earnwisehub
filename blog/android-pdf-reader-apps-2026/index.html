10 Best PDF Reader Apps for Android in 2026 for Reading, Notes & Documents

A good PDF reader app makes it easy to view, annotate and manage PDF documents on Android. These 10 options cover everything from simple viewing to advanced annotation and document editing.

By EarnWiseHub Editorial TeamPublished September 11, 2026

PDFs are one of the most common document formats for contracts, academic papers, ebooks and forms. A reliable PDF reader makes working with these documents on Android much easier. Here are 10 apps worth considering in 2026.

1. Adobe Acrobat Reader

Adobe Acrobat Reader is the original PDF app and remains one of the most comprehensive. The free version handles viewing, basic annotation (highlighting, comments) and form filling. The paid version adds editing, OCR and advanced features.

Why it is useful: The standard PDF app with broad compatibility and reliable rendering.

Best for: Most users who need reliable PDF viewing and basic annotation.

2. Xodo PDF Reader & Editor

Xodo is a free PDF app with strong annotation tools including highlighting, drawing, stamps and text notes. It also supports PDF form filling and digital signatures. Syncs with Google Drive and Dropbox.

Why it is useful: Comprehensive annotation tools at no cost.

Best for: Students and professionals who annotate PDFs heavily.

3. WPS Office

WPS Office is a full office suite that handles PDFs alongside Word, Excel and PowerPoint documents. Useful if you work with multiple document types and want one app for all of them.

Why it is useful: Handles PDFs and office documents in one app.

Best for: Users who work with both PDFs and office documents and prefer one app.

4. Google Drive PDF Viewer

Google Drive opens PDFs directly in-app. For basic viewing and simple annotation, it works well without needing a separate app. Available to all Android users with Google Drive.

Why it is useful: Already installed for most Android users — no additional app needed for basic use.

Best for: Users who only need basic PDF viewing.

5. Foxit PDF Reader

Foxit is a well-established PDF reader with reliable performance. The free version covers reading, annotation and form filling. It integrates with cloud storage services.

Why it is useful: Fast performance and a clean reading interface.

Best for: Users who prioritise reading speed and clean interface.

6. Moon+ Reader

Moon+ Reader is primarily an ebook reader but handles PDFs too. It has extensive customisation options for reading (fonts, colours, brightness, spacing) that make long reading sessions more comfortable.

Why it is useful: Superior reading experience customisation for long documents.

Best for: Users who read long PDF documents and want a comfortable reading environment.

7. PDF Viewer Pro by Pspdfkit

PSPDFKit provides a fast, feature-rich PDF viewer with smooth scrolling and strong annotation tools. The free version has good functionality; a subscription unlocks advanced features.

Why it is useful: Smooth performance with comprehensive annotation.

Best for: Users who need fast, responsive PDF viewing with annotation.

8. Librera Reader

Librera is an open-source reader that handles PDFs, ebooks (EPUB, MOBI) and other formats. It offers detailed reading customisation and is ad-free.

Why it is useful: Open source, multi-format, ad-free.

Best for: Users who read both PDFs and ebooks and want an open-source option.

9. ReadEra

ReadEra reads PDFs alongside many ebook formats with no ads and no internet permission required. It focuses on a clean reading experience without extra features.

Why it is useful: No ads, no internet permission — clean offline reading.

Best for: Privacy-conscious users who want distraction-free offline PDF reading.

10. Dropbox Paper

While not a dedicated PDF app, Dropbox lets you view PDFs stored in your account with comment and annotation capabilities. Worth noting for Dropbox users who access PDFs stored there.

Why it is useful: View and comment on PDFs within Dropbox without a separate app.

Best for: Dropbox users who primarily access PDFs stored in their Dropbox.

Conclusion

Adobe Acrobat Reader and Xodo cover most needs for free. If you annotate PDFs heavily, Xodo's free annotation tools are particularly strong. For long reading sessions, Moon+ Reader or Librera offer better reading comfort. Avoid PDF apps that request excessive permissions or display intrusive ads.
