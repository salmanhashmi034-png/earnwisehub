10 Best Video Editing Apps for Android in 2026

From quick social media clips to more polished short films, these 10 Android video editing apps cover the range from simple trim-and-share tools to multi-track editors.

By EarnWiseHub Editorial TeamPublished September 15, 2026

Video editing on Android has improved significantly. Whether you need to quickly trim and caption a clip for social media or edit a more polished video, there are good tools available. Here are 10 Android video editing apps worth considering in 2026.

1. CapCut

CapCut has become one of the most popular mobile video editing apps, particularly for social media content. It offers templates, auto-captions, text overlays, transitions, music and effects. Free to use with optional paid features.

Why it is useful: Feature-rich for free, excellent for social media content, auto-caption feature saves time.

Best for: Content creators making short-form social media videos.

2. InShot

InShot focuses on simplicity. It handles basic trimming, merging, speed changes, filters, text and music well. It is straightforward enough for beginners while covering the most-needed editing features.

Why it is useful: Simple and fast for everyday video edits.

Best for: Beginners who want quick, simple video edits without a learning curve.

3. Adobe Premiere Rush

Adobe Premiere Rush is a simplified version of Premiere Pro designed for mobile creators. It syncs with desktop Premiere Pro. Good for creators who already use Adobe tools.

Why it is useful: Integrates with Adobe Creative Cloud and desktop Premiere Pro.

Best for: Adobe Creative Cloud subscribers or creators who edit across mobile and desktop.

4. KineMaster

KineMaster is one of the more powerful Android video editors, supporting multi-layer video editing, chroma key (green screen), voice-over recording and professional transitions. A watermark is added in the free version; a subscription removes it.

Why it is useful: Multi-layer editing and chroma key on mobile — more powerful than most mobile editors.

Best for: Creators who need multi-layer editing capabilities on mobile.

5. VN Video Editor

VN (Video Ninja) is free with no watermark. It offers multi-track editing, keyframe animations, speed curves and a range of transitions and effects.

Why it is useful: No watermark on free version, multi-track editing and keyframe support.

Best for: Creators who want capable editing without paying for watermark removal.

6. PowerDirector

PowerDirector from CyberLink is a long-established video editor with a comprehensive feature set. It handles multi-track editing, colour correction, motion tracking and a large effects library.

Why it is useful: Comprehensive features from an established desktop software company.

Best for: Users who want a feature-rich, established mobile editor.

7. Splice

Splice is a simple, clean video editor originally developed by GoPro. It is well-suited to editing action footage and travel videos. Free with optional in-app purchases.

Why it is useful: Clean interface optimised for action and travel footage.

Best for: Action sports and travel content creators.

8. Canva (Video)

Canva's mobile app handles basic video editing for social media content, particularly if you are combining video with graphics and text overlays. Good for creators already using Canva for other content.

Why it is useful: Combines graphic design and video in one app for social media content.

Best for: Canva users who want to add simple video content to their workflow.

9. FilmoraGo

FilmoraGo is the mobile version of Filmora, a popular desktop editor. It offers music, transitions, effects and text overlays. The free version includes a watermark.

Why it is useful: Familiar if you use Filmora on desktop.

Best for: Filmora desktop users who want a connected mobile editing option.

10. Google Photos (Video Editor)

Google Photos includes a basic video editor that handles trimming, stabilisation, filters and simple enhancements. For straightforward edits to phone footage, it is already installed and requires no setup.

Why it is useful: Already installed, handles basic edits without needing a separate app.

Best for: Users who only need basic trimming and stabilisation for personal videos.

Conclusion

CapCut is the most feature-rich free option for social media content. VN is the best choice if you want capable editing without any watermark for free. KineMaster and PowerDirector offer the most advanced features at the cost of a subscription. For beginners, InShot's simplicity is a strong starting point.
