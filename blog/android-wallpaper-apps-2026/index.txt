<p>Your wallpaper is the first thing you see when you unlock your phone. A good wallpaper app gives you access to a wide range of high-quality images that suit your style. Here are 10 Android wallpaper apps worth trying in 2026.</p>

<h2>1. Backdrops</h2>
<p>Backdrops is known for original, artist-created wallpapers rather than stock photography. The design aesthetic tends toward modern and geometric. A free tier offers a good selection; a Pro subscription unlocks everything.</p>
<p><strong>Why it is useful:</strong> Original designs not found elsewhere — your phone looks distinctive.</p>
<p><strong>Best for:</strong> Users who want creative, original artwork rather than photography.</p>

<h2>2. Walli</h2>
<p>Walli features wallpapers created by artists from around the world. The style ranges from illustrations to abstract art. A portion of revenue goes back to contributing artists.</p>
<p><strong>Why it is useful:</strong> Artist-created content with a strong creative and illustrated feel.</p>
<p><strong>Best for:</strong> People who appreciate illustrated and artistic wallpapers.</p>

<h2>3. Zedge</h2>
<p>Zedge is one of the largest wallpaper and ringtone libraries available. It has a huge variety of free content across many styles and categories.</p>
<p><strong>Why it is useful:</strong> Enormous library with something for every preference.</p>
<p><strong>Best for:</strong> Users who want maximum variety and choice.</p>

<h2>4. Unsplash Wallpapers</h2>
<p>Unsplash hosts millions of free high-resolution photographs from photographers worldwide. The Android app makes it easy to browse and set these as wallpapers. All photos are free to use.</p>
<p><strong>Why it is useful:</strong> High-quality photography, all free, enormous library.</p>
<p><strong>Best for:</strong> Users who prefer photography over illustrations.</p>

<h2>5. WallpapersCraft</h2>
<p>WallpapersCraft offers a large collection of wallpapers across categories including nature, architecture, space and abstract. Download is free; images are available in multiple resolutions.</p>
<p><strong>Why it is useful:</strong> Wide variety across many categories, free downloads.</p>
<p><strong>Best for:</strong> Users who want to browse by category to find a specific style.</p>

<h2>6. Muzei Live Wallpaper</h2>
<p>Muzei rotates your wallpaper automatically, blurring it slightly so home screen icons remain readable. It can use artwork from museums, your own photos or third-party plugins as its source.</p>
<p><strong>Why it is useful:</strong> Automatically changes your wallpaper, keeping your phone fresh without manual effort.</p>
<p><strong>Best for:</strong> Users who want their wallpaper to change automatically.</p>

<h2>7. AMOLED / Black Wallpapers</h2>
<p>For AMOLED screen Android phones, true black wallpapers save battery life because AMOLED pixels that display black are turned off entirely. Several dedicated AMOLED wallpaper apps provide black and dark wallpapers optimised for these screens.</p>
<p><strong>Why it is useful:</strong> True black wallpapers extend battery life on AMOLED screen devices.</p>
<p><strong>Best for:</strong> Android phones with AMOLED screens where battery saving is a priority.</p>

<h2>8. Google Photos</h2>
<p>Google Photos lets you set your own photos as wallpapers directly. If you have a favourite photo you have taken yourself, this is the simplest way to use it.</p>
<p><strong>Why it is useful:</strong> Use your own photos — completely personalised.</p>
<p><strong>Best for:</strong> Users who want personal photos as their wallpaper.</p>

<h2>9. Abstruct</h2>
<p>Abstruct was created by a designer and features a curated collection of abstract wallpapers with a consistent artistic style. The free version includes a selection; a paid version unlocks all wallpapers.</p>
<p><strong>Why it is useful:</strong> Curated, consistent aesthetic — all wallpapers look like they belong together.</p>
<p><strong>Best for:</strong> Users who want a visually cohesive, abstract style.</p>

<h2>10. Live Wallpaper Apps</h2>
<p>Various live wallpaper apps offer animated wallpapers — weather effects, particle systems, nature scenes. These consume more battery than static wallpapers. Popular options include <strong>MyerSplash</strong> and <strong>Kustom Live Wallpaper Maker</strong> for those who want to create custom live wallpapers.</p>
<p><strong>Why it is useful:</strong> Animated wallpapers add visual interest to your home screen.</p>
<p><strong>Best for:</strong> Users who want movement on their home screen and are willing to accept slightly higher battery use.</p>

<h2>Conclusion</h2>
<p>Backdrops and Walli are good starting points for original artwork. Unsplash is excellent for photography. If you have an AMOLED screen and care about battery life, using a dark or black wallpaper is a practical choice. All of the options listed offer free content to get started.</p>
