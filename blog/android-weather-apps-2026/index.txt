<p>Weather apps vary significantly in accuracy, design and features. Some focus on clean daily summaries; others offer detailed hourly data, radar maps and severe weather alerts. Here are 10 worth considering for Android in 2026.</p>

<h2>1. Weather Channel</h2>
<p>The Weather Channel app provides reliable forecasts with hourly and 10-day outlooks, radar maps and severe weather alerts. It has a clean design and is widely used.</p>
<p><strong>Why it is useful:</strong> Reliable, detailed forecasts with strong alert system.</p>
<p><strong>Best for:</strong> Users who want a comprehensive, trusted weather source.</p>

<h2>2. AccuWeather</h2>
<p>AccuWeather is known for its "MinuteCast" feature — precipitation forecasts down to the minute for your exact location. Useful for knowing if rain will pass in 10 minutes or last all afternoon.</p>
<p><strong>Why it is useful:</strong> Minute-by-minute precipitation forecasts are more useful than hourly for planning.</p>
<p><strong>Best for:</strong> Users who want precise short-term forecasts for outdoor planning.</p>

<h2>3. Weather Underground</h2>
<p>Weather Underground aggregates data from personal weather stations, giving hyperlocal forecasts that can be more accurate than national weather service data in some areas.</p>
<p><strong>Why it is useful:</strong> Hyperlocal data from nearby personal weather stations.</p>
<p><strong>Best for:</strong> Users who want the most localised possible data for their specific area.</p>

<h2>4. Google Weather</h2>
<p>Google's built-in weather feature (accessible via Google Search or Assistant) provides clean, reliable forecasts. While not a standalone app for most users, it integrates naturally with Android.</p>
<p><strong>Why it is useful:</strong> Always accessible via Google Search without a separate app.</p>
<p><strong>Best for:</strong> Users who only need basic forecasts and prefer not to install another app.</p>

<h2>5. 1Weather</h2>
<p>1Weather offers clean weather summaries with radar maps, hourly forecasts and weather news. It has a widget that integrates well with Android home screens.</p>
<p><strong>Why it is useful:</strong> Clean design with a well-implemented home screen widget.</p>
<p><strong>Best for:</strong> Users who want an aesthetically clean weather app with a good widget.</p>

<h2>6. Windy</h2>
<p>Windy is primarily a wind and weather map app used by pilots, sailors, surfers and outdoor activity enthusiasts. It visualises wind patterns, waves and weather systems on an interactive map.</p>
<p><strong>Why it is useful:</strong> Detailed visualisation of wind, waves and weather patterns.</p>
<p><strong>Best for:</strong> Pilots, sailors, surfers, cyclists and hikers who need detailed wind and weather data.</p>

<h2>7. Appy Weather</h2>
<p>Appy Weather uses multiple weather data sources and presents them in a visually engaging way. It includes a home screen widget and customisable notifications.</p>
<p><strong>Why it is useful:</strong> Visually appealing presentation with multiple data sources.</p>
<p><strong>Best for:</strong> Users who want a visually distinctive weather app.</p>

<h2>8. Today Weather</h2>
<p>Today Weather has a minimal, clean design focused on giving you what you need quickly. It uses multiple weather data providers for accuracy and includes radar maps.</p>
<p><strong>Why it is useful:</strong> Clean, fast interface that gets to the information quickly.</p>
<p><strong>Best for:</strong> Users who want minimal design and fast access to forecasts.</p>

<h2>9. Clime: NOAA Weather Radar Live</h2>
<p>Clime provides real-time weather radar maps alongside standard forecasts. It uses NOAA data (US-focused) for radar accuracy. Useful for tracking storms and severe weather.</p>
<p><strong>Why it is useful:</strong> Real-time radar with clear storm tracking capability.</p>
<p><strong>Best for:</strong> US users who want live radar for storm tracking.</p>

<h2>10. Weather & Radar</h2>
<p>Weather & Radar from the makers of wetter.com provides detailed European and global forecasts with radar maps, satellite imagery and weather warnings.</p>
<p><strong>Why it is useful:</strong> Strong European data with detailed radar and warnings.</p>
<p><strong>Best for:</strong> European users who want detailed forecasts and radar.</p>

<h2>Conclusion</h2>
<p>AccuWeather's MinuteCast feature stands out for short-term planning. Weather Underground offers the most localised data. For outdoor activities involving wind or water, Windy is particularly useful. Most apps have free tiers — try one for a week and see if its forecasts match actual conditions in your area before committing to a paid tier.</p>
