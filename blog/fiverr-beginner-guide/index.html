Fiverr Beginner Guide: How Gigs Work, Fees & Getting Your First Order

Fiverr works differently from most freelance platforms — sellers create service listings rather than applying for jobs. This guide explains how Fiverr works and how to set up your first gig.

By EarnWiseHub Editorial TeamPublished August 12, 2026Updated September 20, 2026

Fiverr operates differently from most freelance platforms. Instead of browsing job posts and submitting applications, you create service listings (called "Gigs") and wait for buyers to find and purchase them. This guide explains how that model works in practice.

1. What is Fiverr?

Fiverr is a marketplace where freelancers (called sellers) create service listings that buyers can purchase directly. The name comes from the original concept of $5 services, though services now range widely in price.

Categories include writing, design, video, music, programming, business, lifestyle and more. Almost any skill that can be delivered digitally can potentially be offered as a Gig.

2. How Gigs work

As a seller, you define exactly what you will provide, at what price, and within what timeframe. A Gig typically has three tiers:

  • Basic: The minimum service at the lowest price
  • Standard: More work, more features, higher price
  • Premium: Full service at the top price

You can also add Gig Extras — optional add-ons buyers can purchase alongside a Gig (for example: faster delivery, additional revisions).

When a buyer purchases your Gig, you receive an order notification and the work begins. You deliver the work by the deadline you set, the buyer accepts it, and Fiverr releases payment.

3. Fees

Fiverr charges sellers a service fee on every order. As of the last check, this was 20% of the order value. This means a £100 order results in £80 paid to the seller.

Buyers also pay a service fee on top of the listed price. This means the cost to a buyer is higher than the price shown on your Gig.

Always check Fiverr's Help Center for current fee information, as rates can change.

4. Seller levels

Fiverr has a seller level system that affects your visibility and perks:

  • New Seller: All new accounts start here
  • Level 1: Unlocked after completing a certain number of orders with good ratings
  • Level 2: For established sellers with a strong track record
  • Top Rated Seller: Fiverr's highest standard seller badge, manually reviewed
  • Pro Verified: A separate verification programme for professional sellers

Higher levels improve your positioning in search results. As a new seller, expect lower visibility than established sellers in the same category. This is one of the key challenges of starting on Fiverr.

5. Creating an effective Gig

The quality of your Gig listing significantly affects whether buyers contact you:

  • Title: Be specific. "I will write a 1,000-word blog post for your tech startup" is more searchable and targeted than "I will write blog posts."
  • Category and tags: Choose accurately — these affect where you appear in search
  • Description: Write clearly about what you deliver, what is included in each tier, and what the buyer should expect. Address common buyer questions proactively.
  • Pricing: Research what comparable Gigs in your category charge. Setting a competitive but not desperately low price is usually more effective than racing to the bottom.
  • Images: Use clean, professional images. Design-related sellers often show portfolio samples. Service sellers can use a well-designed infographic.
  • Video: Gigs with an introductory video reportedly convert better. A short, clear, professional video explanation of your service can help.
  • Requirements: List exactly what information you need from a buyer before you can start. This saves messages back and forth.

6. Getting first orders

New sellers with no reviews face a visibility disadvantage. Practical approaches:

  • Optimise your Gig for specific search terms buyers use
  • Share your Gig link on professional social media, LinkedIn, or communities where potential buyers are active
  • Set competitive pricing initially — not zero, but realistic for someone building a track record
  • Deliver early orders to an excellent standard to earn 5-star reviews as quickly as possible
  • Respond to buyer messages promptly — response rate affects your visibility

It can take weeks to receive a first order. This is normal for new sellers.

7. Payments

Fiverr holds earnings for a clearing period after order completion before they become available for withdrawal. The clearing period varies based on seller level. Withdrawal options include PayPal, bank transfer, Payoneer and other methods depending on your country. Minimum withdrawal amounts apply.

Check Fiverr's current payment documentation for exact clearing periods and withdrawal options for your region.

8. Pros and limitations

Pros

  • You set the terms, price and scope of your service upfront
  • No bidding on individual jobs — clients come to you
  • Large international buyer base
  • Good for repeatable, standardised services

Limitations

  • 20% service fee reduces earnings noticeably
  • New sellers have low search visibility — building momentum takes time
  • Highly competitive in popular categories
  • Clearing period means you wait before accessing earnings
  • Some categories are oversaturated with very low-priced competition

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Fiverr free to use as a seller?

Creating a seller account is free. Fiverr takes 20% of each order as a service fee. There are no upfront costs to create Gigs.

What is the minimum age to sell on Fiverr?

Sellers must be at least 13 years old, with parental consent required for users under 18 in some jurisdictions. Check Fiverr's current terms for the full details.

Can I sell on Fiverr from any country?

Fiverr is available in many countries, but payment options vary by region. Check Fiverr's current country support and payment method availability.

What types of services sell well on Fiverr?

Services that are clearly defined, repeatable and easily delivered digitally tend to work well. This includes writing, design, video editing, voiceovers, programming and marketing services. Vague or overly complex service descriptions tend to underperform.

Conclusion

Fiverr suits freelancers who offer clearly defined, repeatable services and are willing to invest time in optimising their Gig listings and building early reviews. The 20% fee and new-seller visibility challenges are real factors to weigh when deciding if it is the right platform for your situation.
