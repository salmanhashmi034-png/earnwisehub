<p>A personal portfolio website gives potential clients and employers a single place to see your work, understand your skills and get in touch. For most freelancers and online workers, it is more effective than any platform profile alone. This guide covers how to build one that works.</p>

<h2>What to include</h2>
<p>A simple portfolio website needs:</p>
<ul>
  <li><strong>Your name and what you do:</strong> Make this immediately clear. Visitors should understand who you are and what you offer within seconds of arriving.</li>
  <li><strong>3–6 portfolio samples:</strong> Your best, most relevant work. Less is more — quality over quantity.</li>
  <li><strong>A short about section:</strong> Brief professional bio, your background, what types of work you take on.</li>
  <li><strong>Contact details or a contact form:</strong> Make it easy for people to reach you.</li>
  <li><strong>Optional: testimonials from clients</strong> (only genuine ones)</li>
  <li><strong>Optional: a services or rates section</strong></li>
</ul>
<p>You do not need to include everything at once. A simple, clear site with good samples performs better than a complex, cluttered one.</p>

<h2>Platform options</h2>

<h3>No-code / website builders (easiest)</h3>
<ul>
  <li><strong>Squarespace:</strong> Clean, professional templates. Paid subscription. Popular with creatives.</li>
  <li><strong>Wix:</strong> Flexible drag-and-drop builder. Free tier available with Wix subdomain.</li>
  <li><strong>WordPress.com:</strong> Free tier available. More customisation options than Wix at higher tiers.</li>
  <li><strong>Carrd.co:</strong> Very simple single-page sites. Free tier available. Good for a clean, minimal presence.</li>
</ul>

<h3>Portfolio-specific platforms</h3>
<ul>
  <li><strong>Behance:</strong> Free. Specifically designed for portfolios. Well-known in creative industries.</li>
  <li><strong>Contently:</strong> Designed for writers. Free for participants.</li>
  <li><strong>GitHub Pages:</strong> Free hosting for developers who know HTML/CSS.</li>
</ul>

<h3>Self-hosted (most flexible, requires more effort)</h3>
<ul>
  <li><strong>WordPress.org + hosting:</strong> Most flexible option. Requires purchasing hosting and a domain (typically $5–15/month combined). Greater control over design and functionality.</li>
</ul>
<p>For beginners, starting with a free tier on Carrd, Wix or a platform-specific portfolio site is practical. Upgrading to a custom domain later is straightforward.</p>

<h2>Domain name</h2>
<p>A custom domain (yourname.com) looks more professional than a subdomain (yourname.wix.com). Domains typically cost $10–15 per year. Common registrars include Namecheap, Google Domains and Cloudflare. A custom domain is optional for a starting portfolio but worth adding as you build your professional presence.</p>

<h2>Design principles for a simple portfolio</h2>
<ul>
  <li><strong>Clarity first:</strong> What you do should be obvious immediately</li>
  <li><strong>Clean layout:</strong> White space is not wasted space — it makes content easier to read</li>
  <li><strong>Readable typography:</strong> Stick to 1–2 font families. Make body text large enough to read comfortably.</li>
  <li><strong>Mobile-friendly:</strong> Most visitors will view it on mobile. Test on a phone before publishing.</li>
  <li><strong>Fast loading:</strong> Compress images before uploading. Slow sites create poor impressions.</li>
  <li><strong>No dead links:</strong> Check all links work before sharing your URL anywhere</li>
</ul>

<h2>Presenting portfolio samples</h2>
<p>For each portfolio piece:</p>
<ul>
  <li>Show the work clearly (screenshot, image, live link, PDF)</li>
  <li>Briefly explain the context: what was the brief, what did you deliver</li>
  <li>If the work produced measurable results, include them — but only if genuine</li>
</ul>

<h2>Getting your portfolio seen</h2>
<p>Once your portfolio is live:</p>
<ul>
  <li>Add the URL to your LinkedIn profile, freelance platform profiles, and email signature</li>
  <li>Share it when relevant in professional conversations</li>
  <li>Update it regularly as you complete new work</li>
</ul>

<h2>Conclusion</h2>
<p>A simple, focused online portfolio with good work samples is one of the most effective tools available to a freelancer or online worker. You do not need design expertise or technical skills to build one — the tools available today make it accessible to anyone willing to invest a few hours in setting it up.</p>
