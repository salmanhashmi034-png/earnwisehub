<p>Respondent.io is a research recruitment platform that connects researchers with participants for interviews, focus groups and usability studies. It is distinguished from general survey platforms primarily by its higher pay rates — made possible by the professional profiles many studies require.</p>

<p><em>Information last checked: September 2026. Verify current details at <a href="https://www.respondent.io" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">respondent.io</a> before signing up.</em></p>

<div class="bg-blue-50 border border-blue-200 rounded-lg p-4 my-6">
  <h3 class="font-semibold text-blue-900 mb-2">Quick Summary</h3>
  <ul class="text-blue-800 space-y-1">
    <li>✓ Higher pay per study than most platforms</li>
    <li>✓ Legitimate professional research platform</li>
    <li>✗ Many studies require specific professional backgrounds</li>
    <li>✗ Studies are not available daily — availability varies</li>
    <li>✗ Screening process is competitive</li>
  </ul>
</div>

<h2>What is Respondent?</h2>
<p>Respondent is a platform designed to connect researchers — typically from technology companies and market research firms — with participants who have specific professional backgrounds or experiences. Studies tend to pay significantly more than general survey platforms because they require specific expertise.</p>
<p>Examples of the types of backgrounds researchers recruit for include: software engineers, HR managers, marketing professionals, small business owners, healthcare workers and financial professionals. Consumer studies (not requiring professional backgrounds) are also available but are fewer.</p>

<h2>How it works</h2>
<ol>
  <li>Create a free participant account on respondent.io</li>
  <li>Complete your profile, including your professional background and employment history</li>
  <li>Browse available studies that match your profile</li>
  <li>Apply to studies you are eligible for — a screening questionnaire is typically required</li>
  <li>If selected, you are scheduled for a video interview or other session</li>
  <li>Complete the session</li>
  <li>Receive payment (typically via PayPal)</li>
</ol>

<h2>Pay and payments</h2>
<p>Respondent studies typically pay more per hour than general survey platforms — consumer studies may pay around $30–$75 per session, while specialist professional studies can pay more. These figures are illustrative based on publicly discussed rates and may not reflect current or future rates.</p>
<p><strong>Do not make financial plans based on these figures.</strong> Always check the payment listed for each specific study before applying, and verify current platform policies at respondent.io.</p>
<p>Payment is typically via PayPal. A holding period applies between session completion and payment release.</p>

<h2>Who Respondent suits</h2>
<p>Respondent works well for:</p>
<ul>
  <li>Professionals in technology, business, marketing, healthcare or finance who want to participate in research occasionally</li>
  <li>People with specific experiences researchers are looking for</li>
  <li>Those who do not mind a lower frequency of studies in exchange for higher per-session pay</li>
</ul>
<p>It is less suitable for people without a professional background looking for frequent study availability. General consumer studies do exist, but the majority of higher-paying studies target specific professional profiles.</p>

<h2>Pros and limitations</h2>

<h3>Pros</h3>
<ul>
  <li>Higher per-session pay than most survey platforms</li>
  <li>Research involves meaningful professional conversations, not just clicking through questions</li>
  <li>Free to join and use as a participant</li>
</ul>

<h3>Limitations</h3>
<ul>
  <li>Most high-paying studies require specific professional backgrounds</li>
  <li>Application screening is competitive — applying does not guarantee selection</li>
  <li>Study availability is not constant</li>
  <li>Not designed as a regular income source</li>
</ul>

<h2>Frequently Asked Questions</h2>

<h3>Is Respondent available worldwide?</h3>
<p>Respondent operates primarily in English-speaking markets. Country availability can change — check respondent.io for current information about participant eligibility in your country.</p>

<h3>How often are studies available?</h3>
<p>This varies considerably depending on your profile. Professionals in high-demand fields may find more studies available to them than those with more general backgrounds.</p>

<h3>Is Respondent free for participants?</h3>
<p>Yes, creating a participant account is free.</p>

<h2>Conclusion</h2>
<p>Respondent is a legitimate research platform best suited to professionals looking to occasionally earn from research participation. The higher pay rates make it worth registering if you have a relevant professional background — but it is not a high-frequency income source for most participants.</p>
